# Pricing — ceyonai

ceyonai is an AI captioning and video editor for Tamil, Thanglish and Indic short-form creators. There is one free tier plus a single full-access toolkit sold at five durations — every paid plan unlocks the same features (4K export, no watermark, all export formats, full translation) and differs only in price, included processing minutes, and how long access lasts. All prices in INR.

## Free
- Price: ₹0
- Processing: 15 minutes / month
- Storage: 1 GB
- Max export resolution: 720p
- Exports: MP4 only, up to 2 exports, with a ceyonai watermark
- Translation: preview only (edit, no translated export)
- Project retention: 7 days

## Day Pass
- Price: ₹59 (one-off, 1 day of access)
- Processing: 45 minutes
- Storage: 2 GB
- Max export resolution: 4K (2160p)
- Exports: all formats (MP4, SRT, VTT, ASS, FCPXML, EDL), no watermark
- Translation: full (edit and export translated tracks)

## Monthly
- Price: ₹499 (30 days)
- Processing: 300 minutes
- Storage: 30 GB
- Full toolkit: 4K, no watermark, all formats, full translation

## 3 Months
- Price: ₹1199 (90 days)
- Processing: 900 minutes
- Full toolkit: 30 GB, 4K, no watermark, all formats, full translation

## 6 Months
- Price: ₹2199 (180 days)
- Processing: 1800 minutes
- Full toolkit: 30 GB, 4K, no watermark, all formats, full translation

## 1 Year
- Price: ₹3999 (365 days)
- Processing: 3600 minutes
- Full toolkit: 30 GB, 4K, no watermark, all formats, full translation
